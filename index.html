No black box

What happens
under the surface

The playground deliberately uses a small, fallible method. The point is to inspect where it works—and catch where it doesn’t.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Your browser captures at most four seconds after you explicitly allow the microphone.

  2. 02

    Summarize

    Web Audio turns the clip into loudness, frequency bands, and an MFCC-like cosine summary.

  3. 03

    Compare

    A nearest-neighbor baseline measures Euclidean distance between normalized spectral fingerprints.

  4. 04

    Question

    You inspect every neighbor and mark wrong guesses. No hidden training and no cloud model.
