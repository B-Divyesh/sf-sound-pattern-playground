Terms of use

A learning tool, not a judge.

Use Sound Pattern Playground to explore audio features and the behavior of a small nearest-neighbor baseline. By using it, you agree to the boundaries below.

Important: features and guesses are illustrative, fallible, and not diagnostic. Never rely on them for medical, safety-critical, legal, identity, or sensitive-trait decisions.

Acceptable use

  • Record only sounds you have the right and permission to capture.
  • Use action or object labels rather than identities, emotions, health conditions, or personal traits.
  • Do not use the tool for surveillance, covert monitoring, or consequential decisions about people.

Your data

You own the recordings and datasets you create. They remain on your device unless you export and share them. You are responsible for keeping exported files secure and obtaining consent from anyone whose sound may be captured.
